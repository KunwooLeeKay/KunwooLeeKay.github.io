Hi 👋, I'm Kunwoo Lee — a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, where I'm developing next-generation AI systems that make human-movement analysis more accessible and clinically meaningful. My research sits at the exciting intersection of computer vision, sensor fusion, and biomechanical modeling that can understand human motion in ways previously thought impossible.

At CMU, I joined the Musculoskeletal Biomechanics Lab under Prof. Eni Halilaj, where I work on projects pushing the boundaries of biomechanics and AI. Our mission is to build AI systems that don’t just analyze human movement — but make that analysis accessible, clinically relevant, and transformative for researchers and patients alike.

Research Experience — Master’s

Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA

Generative AI to recover full-body kinematics from sparse sensors (Meta Quest Pro); extensible to RGB/IMU; robust to single-leg/OOD motions.

Calibration-Free Multimodal Physical Therapy (PT) Assessment

Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA

Validating Computer Vision-based Human Kinetics Estimation

Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA

sEMG-Based Keyboards-Free Typing with Deep Learning

Research Experience — Undergraduate

Illinois Institute of Technology, Chicago, IL

Illinois Institute of Technology, Chicago, IL
