About Me

Hi 👋, I'm Kunwoo Lee — a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, where I'm developing next-generation AI systems that make human-movement analysis more accessible and clinically meaningful.

At CMU, I joined the Musculoskeletal Biomechanics Lab under Prof. Eni Halilaj, where I work on projects pushing the boundaries of biomechanics and AI. My research sits at the exciting intersection of computer vision, sensor fusion, and biomechanical modeling that can understand human motion in ways previously thought impossible.

My research interests are two-fold: First, I aim to develop robust, accessible movement analysis systems that bridge the gap between engineering innovation and clinical practice—creating tools that work not just in research labs, but in real-world clinics with constrained resources. Second, I'm passionate about translating these insights into wearable assistive technologies such as exoskeletons and prosthetics that actively support recovery and restore mobility. My ultimate goal is to ensure that biomechanical innovations reach every patient who needs them, from well-resourced hospitals to small community clinics.
