EMG→QWERTY: Towards Keyboards-Free Typing via sEMG

Exploring Transformer/Conformer encoders for mapping wrist sEMG signals to virtual keyboard input. Based on datasets open-sourced by Meta: Surface Electromyography Datasets (NeurIPS 2024) .

sEMG Conformer/Transformer CTC HCI

We explore surface electromyography (sEMG) as a typing interface for spatial/VR computing, replacing physical keyboards with muscle-driven input. Using a large public dataset and attention-based encoders, our causal, online models reduce character error rate (CER) from a TDS baseline to 20.34% (online), without personalization. The approach preserves the familiar “typing” mental model while removing hardware constraints.

Why this matters

Gaze or hand-ray input in head-mounted displays is slow and fatiguing. sEMG offers silent, head-up, eyes-free text entry that can work without a desk. The challenge: high inter-user variability, sensor placement shifts, and the need for low-latency decoding that still captures co-articulation and temporal context in muscle activity.

Dataset (emg2qwerty)

We use a large-scale dataset of non-invasive wrist sEMG for touch typing (108 users, ~346 hours). Signals are recorded at 2 kHz from 32 channels (16 per hand). We visualize inter-channel correlations, note strong adjacency effects (up to ~2 neighbors), and apply augmentations that promote rotational and temporal robustness (band rotation, temporal alignment jitter, SpecAugment).

Methods in 60 seconds

  • Input & features: 32-ch sEMG → log-spectrograms with normalization and rotation-invariant adaptation layers.
  • Encoders: Baseline TDS; our variants add a Transformer layer stack or replace with a compact Conformer encoder.
  • Training: CTC loss; online/causal setup for real-time use; augmentation for user/session variability.
  • Decoding: Greedy or beam search; optional LMs (6-gram char LM, Flan-T5, GPT-4-turbo) for correction.
  • Metrics: CER overall and by error type (subs/ins/del); online (streaming) vs. offline modes.

Models

TDS (Baseline) — Efficient conv blocks with ~1 s receptive field; rotation-invariant pre-module and SpecAugment.

Transformer Encoder — 4× projection bottleneck (e.g., 768→192) before a compact Transformer; self-attention captures long-range dependencies to disambiguate similar muscle patterns.

Conformer Encoder — 3 layers / 6 heads; fuses local convolution with global attention. Tuned for short sequences and multi-channel input.

Future work

  • Causality: Converting the acausal baseline to causal only slightly increased CER, supporting real-time feasibility.
  • Latency target: Current online windowing adds delay; aim for ≤50–100 ms using short stride windows with long padded context.
  • Prediction: Exploit EMG leading motion by tens of ms for anticipatory decoding.
  • Generalization: Continue adapting to user variability (rotation-invariant layers, robust normalization, session adaptation).
