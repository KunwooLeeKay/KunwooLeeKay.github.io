Sparse-Sensor Motion Tracking for Accessible Biomechanics

Using VR (Meta Quest Pro) for motion tracking.

Generative AI Biomechanics Movement Analysis

As a mission to push biomechanics further into real-world applications, where it is even more accessible, preferrably with extremely sparse set of sensors, i.e. two hand held controllers and a headset, we are working with Meta Quest Pro to generate full body human motion from their built-in IMUs. This work is in collaboration with Emory University. Building on some preliminary work achieved with Meta, we are now working on a more robust system that can handle more variety type of movements that are out-of-distribution such as single-legged exercises.
